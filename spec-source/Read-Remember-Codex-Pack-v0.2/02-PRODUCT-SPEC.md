# Product Specification

## Working name
Read & Remember

## Problem
The target user can understand short pieces of text but often recalls only one or two points. He prefers reading in small chunks. The app should help him practise useful recall strategies while keeping reading comfortable and adult.

## Primary outcome
Improve the user's ability to understand and later retrieve useful ideas from material he chooses to read.

## Secondary outcomes
- discover a comfortable chunk size;
- reduce dependence on clues;
- retain information over longer delays;
- learn which strategies help;
- test whether gains transfer to fresh reading.

## Core session
1. User pastes or opens a piece of text.
2. App creates a coherent small chunk.
3. App identifies 1–3 source-backed key ideas.
4. User reads at his own pace.
5. User chooses "I've read it".
6. Text hides.
7. User speaks or types what he remembers.
8. Transcript is confirmed if speech recognition may have changed meaning.
9. Scorer evaluates meaning against the source.
10. App acknowledges what was remembered.
11. If needed, app moves through the clue ladder.
12. Each key idea receives a memory-state update.
13. Scheduler recommends the next retrieval time.
14. In shadow mode, recommendations are logged but cannot change the live schedule.
15. Later, review begins with retrieval before showing the answer.

## Two modes
### Help me read this
Comprehension first. Source remains easy to reveal. Definitions, read-aloud and simple explanations are allowed.

### Help me remember this
Adds hidden-text recall, clue ladder and delayed review.

## Progress metrics
- immediate independent recall;
- next-day independent recall;
- 7-day independent recall;
- clue dependence;
- comprehension;
- comfortable chunk size;
- fresh-text transfer;
- strategy effectiveness.

Never show an IQ, brain age or clinical score.

## Non-goals
- diagnosis;
- symptom detection;
- clinical monitoring;
- medication advice;
- social competition;
- generic brain games;
- an open-ended AI companion.

## Accessibility
- clear single-task screens;
- large tap targets;
- dynamic text;
- no timers by default;
- no streak penalties;
- type or speak;
- no auto-advance;
- source always recoverable;
- calm adult visual design.
